Adoption by practice

Health is green when at least 70% of seats were active and no more than 10% of the week's threads stalled, yellow at 40% and 25%, red otherwise. Stalled threads are counted as a share of the week's volume, so a busy practice is not punished for its size.

How it decides

FlagRuleWhy it matters
stalledSomeone outside the firm has not answered two follow-ups in 48 hoursMore than half of the waiting in a search is follow-ups nobody answered, so a person should call or text
stalledWaiting on the firm's own partner or recruiter for 24 hoursInternal waits are the easiest to fix and the most embarrassing to leave
stalledNothing sent to someone outside the firm for 30 hours while waiting on themThe 24-hour cadence was missed, which points at a product issue to reproduce and send to engineering
stalledThe scheduling agent has not acted on its turn for an hourSomething is stuck in the product, not with a person
at riskMore than 12 rounds and still openA public search-scheduling benchmark shows cancellations rising from 3.9% to 16.4% past twelve rounds
at riskThe client has moved the meeting twiceEach move restarts the negotiation; firm backup slots stop the loop
at riskWaiting 24 hours on someone outside the firmWorth watching before it becomes a stall

Every threshold is a constant in stallmon/rules.py so it can be tuned per customer. The rules are checked in CI against 28 hand-labeled threads with golive-gate: every stalled thread must be caught, with at most one false alarm. Go-live gate report (28 labeled threads).
